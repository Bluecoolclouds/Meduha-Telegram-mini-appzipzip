---
name: Generated video privacy
description: Privacy and sharing rules for generated Genjutsu and Seedance videos in Meduha.
---

Generated Genjutsu and Seedance videos stay private in the creator's account unless the creator explicitly chooses to publish them in Community.

**Why:** The user required an explicit sharing action so generated results are not published automatically.

**How to apply:** Keep generation completion, storage, recent-run history, and playback private. Only a separate user-triggered publishing action may create a Community post.
