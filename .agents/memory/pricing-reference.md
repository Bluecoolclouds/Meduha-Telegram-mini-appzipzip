The user explicitly chose prices like Higgsfield rather than retaining Meduha's earlier ruble prices.

**Why:** The user selected «Сделать цены как на Higgsfield» after being told that Higgsfield uses USD subscriptions while Meduha had ruble one-time packages.

**How to apply:** Use verified monthly USD reference prices, identify the billing period, and do not treat an annual-billing promotional monthly equivalent as a one-time purchase. Keep payment and subscription activation explicitly unavailable until implemented. Matching prices does not authorize copying Higgsfield website-only unlimited benefits or changing generation model prices.

Offer 1 month, 3 months, and 1 year; the 3-month percentage discount is half of the annual percentage discount.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested «1 месяц/3 месяца/1 год» and half the annual discount for the 3-month term.

**How to apply:** Show the monthly equivalent alongside the total for the selected term; credit allowances remain monthly. Calculate from the verified base-tier annual price rather than a rounded advertised discount badge. If extending a base-tier percentage to unverified larger Ultra volumes, disclose it as Meduha's calculation, not a confirmed Higgsfield quote.

Public pricing HTML and a direct browser may not expose dynamic volume prices. Official Higgsfield articles can document Ultra volume prices, but their base-plan information may be older than the pricing page.

**Why:** Pricing extraction exposed only the selected Ultra volume; direct browser loading and static asset requests did not expose other options. An official Seedance pricing article documented the volume amounts while reporting older Starter/Plus allowances.

**How to apply:** Verify each price against its relevant official source, prefer the current pricing page for base tiers, retain source links, and never invent larger-volume prices by multiplying a base price.

Choosing a tariff card or pressing «Продолжить» must open payment-method
selection directly, without an intermediate «Проверка выбора» confirmation.

**Why:** the user explicitly requested this shorter tariff flow.

**How to apply:** keep the chosen tariff and billing term when returning from
payment methods. Carousel gestures and credit-volume controls select options
without advancing to payment.
